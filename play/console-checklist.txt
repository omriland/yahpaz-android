Play Console steps after Google finishes identity verification.

Blocked until verification is complete
Google will not accept an app for review until the developer account is verified.
https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10841920

Also blocked for a new personal account
Production (public listing) requires closed testing with 12 testers who opted in, for 14 consecutive days, then an application for production access. Internal testing can go live to invited emails much sooner.

When verification clears

1. Create the app
   - Create app → name אבן דרך, default language Hebrew, app (not game), free
   - Package name MUST stay com.yahpz.responder. It cannot change later.
   - Declarations: no ads, not a news app, not a government app unless you later decide it is

2. Store listing
   - Paste play/listing-he.txt
   - Upload play/assets/icon-512.png
   - Upload play/assets/feature-graphic-1024x500.png
   - Upload at least 2 phone screenshots
   - Privacy policy URL: https://yahpz.com/privacy

3. App access (login wall)
   - All or some functionality is restricted
   - Add a reviewer username + password for a real unit-test account
   - Without this, review will reject

4. Ads: No
5. Content rating: start IARC questionnaire. Utility / productivity, no user-to-public UGC, no violence. Users do interact inside a closed unit — answer that honestly as private / restricted users.
6. Target audience: 18+
7. News: No
8. COVID: No
9. Data safety: play/data-safety.txt
10. Government / political: No unless you classify the unit app that way
11. Financial features: No

12. App signing
   - First AAB upload: choose Play App Signing, let Google generate the app signing key
   - Upload dist/com.yahpz.responder-release.aab (built by scripts/build-aab.sh)
   - Keep keystore/play-upload.jks + key.properties forever. That is the upload key.

13. First release track
   - Prefer Internal testing (up to 100 emails). Closest to TestFlight.
   - Then Closed testing if you want the 14-day production clock to start.
   - Do not send to Production on a new personal account until Play lets you apply.

14. Device verification
   - New personal accounts must confirm a real Android device in the Play Console app.

Build commands
  scripts/create-upload-keystore.sh   # once
  scripts/build-aab.sh                # signed AAB → dist/
